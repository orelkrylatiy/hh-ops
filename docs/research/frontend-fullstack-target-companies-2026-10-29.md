# Frontend / Full-stack target companies in Russia

Research date: 29-30 October 2026 (Asia/Yekaterinburg)

## Purpose and verification rules

This is a working document for a company-by-company review of 30 target employers. The scope is limited to frontend, full-stack, interface-development and closely related web roles.

- An opening is marked **active** only when it is present on the employer's official career site on the research date.
- Archived openings are not treated as available; they may be retained only as evidence of a company's stack and hiring pattern.
- A company career page or a generic resume form is not evidence of a current frontend opening.
- No applications, forms, emails or messages are sent during this research.
- Salary is recorded only when the employer publishes it.
